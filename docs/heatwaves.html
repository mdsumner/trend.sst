Marine heatwaves (Hobday et al. 2016) computed per pixel on GHRSST MUR at its native 0.01°, for regional deep dives. A pixel is in a heatwave when its SST stays above the 90th percentile of its own seasonal climatology for 5+ days. Baseline: MUR's own 2003–2025; OISST on the classic 1982–2011 baseline is shown alongside, never mixed in. How this is computed.
